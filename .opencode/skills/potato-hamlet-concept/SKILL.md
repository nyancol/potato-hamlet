# Potato Hamlet Concept

This skill contains the canonical description of the Potato Hamlet project.
Refer to it when designing, discussing, or implementing anything for Potato Hamlet
to stay aligned with the original vision.

## What Problem Are We Trying to Solve?

Modern democratic societies often struggle; people may feel unheard, and the balance
between community, individual, and environmental interests can seem broken.

This project, 'Potato Hamlet,' is inspired by the vision of the 'Potato Group' –
a small group of friends dreaming of an alternative: living together in a small hamlet,
building an eco-friendly village governed by a renewed democratic mindset that actively
seeks this balance, far from the chaos of modern life.

## How Do We Want to Solve This Problem?

Solving these problems requires the active contribution of all participants:
proposals, actions, and feedback.

For any solution to be trusted, at the design and implementation phase, it must be
grounded in documented facts, whether it is in the physics that describes how our
ecosystem works but also in the honest participant feedback that seeks the best
rather than the worst.

## What Is the Potato Hamlet Simulation Game?

The Potato Hamlet game is a simulation open-world multiplayer game and is an attempt
at creating a virtual world where participants can experiment with new ways of living
together, in a world that follows at best the rules of the real world.

### Onboarding

At first, participants spawn in a remote countryside, far from civilisation, with only
a backpack containing items that the user has selected before onboarding on the journey.
They are then welcomed by the Spirit of the Hamlet, which explains the core concepts
of the game.

### Survival and Environment

Your player has fundamental needs that mirror real-world survival: you must manage
hunger by eating, maintain energy through sleep, and stay healthy with regular exercise.
You can gather natural resources like wood from trees or stone from quarries to build
your home and other structures.

Just like in reality, your choices have consequences; eating too little or too much of
certain nutrients can impact your health and performance, and neglecting sleep or
exercise will have similar effects.

Furthermore, your interaction with the environment is crucial. Over-harvesting resources
or polluting the land can negatively impact the local biodiversity and the long-term
sustainability of the ecosystem.

These environmental metrics, such as soil health, water purity, and nutrients intake,
are tracked and displayed within the game through the Spirit of the Hamlet. It serves
as a guide, offering insights and data to help you understand the impact of your actions
on both your personal well-being and the health of the surrounding environment,
encouraging sustainable practices.

### Customization and Identity

Players have extensive freedom to customize the aesthetic of their creations, including
houses, fences, decorations, and even the overall look of the Hamlet for special events
like Christmas.

This customization goes beyond simple in-game options; players can express their
individual style and creativity through various means, such as uploading their own 2D
drawings to be used as textures or design of buildings, importing 3D models created in
external software like Blender for unique structures, contributing pieces of music to
the game's soundscape, or even using AI prompts to generate unique visual elements.

This visual and auditory expression is a key part of a player's identity within the
Hamlet and how they contribute to the community's shared space. However, this creative
freedom interacts with the community's chosen governance system, meaning certain types
or locations of customization may be subject to community approval or specific roles
depending on the established rules.

### Governance – The Constitution

A defining moment occurs early in the game: on day one, all participants convene within
a dedicated virtual space (e.g., an in-game forum or meeting area) to collaboratively
draft and agree on a community constitution.

This crucial step establishes the rules and structure of their governance. Once consensus
is reached and the constitution is adopted, the main simulation phase begins.

Players are then expected to adhere to the constitutional rules they established. These
community-defined rules govern player actions, from resource management and building
permissions to how private/public property aesthetics are managed.

To assist, the Spirit of the Hamlet provides guidance, helping players understand and
follow the procedures outlined in their constitution (e.g., how to propose a law, hold
an election, or resolve disputes according to their agreed system).

## When Applying This Skill

- Ground game mechanics in real-world physics / ecology where feasible.
- Balance three poles: community, individual, environment.
- Treat participant feedback (proposals, actions, feedback) as a first-class input.
- Use the Spirit of the Hamlet as the in-game guide for survival stats, environmental
  metrics, and constitutional procedures.
- Any building, resource, customization, or governance feature must respect the
  community constitution flow: draft on day one, then enforce.
